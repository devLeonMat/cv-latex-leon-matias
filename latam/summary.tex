\section{Professional Summary}

Full Stack Engineer building production-grade web and mobile applications \textbf{since 2014},
with a career centred on \textbf{Latin American banking, insurance, and retail}. Strong on the
backend --- \textbf{Java / Spring Boot} microservices, \textbf{Spring WebFlux} reactive services,
and \textbf{Node.js / NestJS} REST APIs on \textbf{AWS}, \textbf{Azure}, and \textbf{GCP} --- and
equally at home in \textbf{Angular} and \textbf{React} front ends and \textbf{React Native} mobile
apps.

\vspace{3pt}

Delivered for institutions across Peru, Paraguay, and the Caribbean: \textbf{Scotiabank Peru},
\textbf{BanBif}, \textbf{National Commercial Bank Jamaica}, \textbf{Banco GNB Paraguay},
\textbf{RIMAC Seguros}, \textbf{SURA}, \textbf{Intercorp Retail}, \textbf{Equifax}, and
\textbf{Olva Courier} --- plus SambaSafety and a U.S. health insurer on the North American side. Experienced in microservices, event-driven systems, hexagonal architecture,
cloud-native delivery, and CI/CD under the regulatory and availability constraints that banking and
insurance impose.

\vspace{3pt}

Career history in this document is reconciled against the \textbf{Certificado Único Laboral} issued
by Peru's Ministry of Labour: every salaried Peruvian engagement matches an official payroll record,
and the 2015--2017 freelance period falls outside payroll by definition.
